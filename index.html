Who We Are

A Recruitment Partner Built for Modern Businesses

UrbanStaff Partners is a modern staffing and recruitment firm helping businesses across India streamline hiring and workforce acquisition. We work with startups, growing companies, BPOs, retail businesses, and mid-sized organizations.

Our work is straightforward: understand your requirement, find people who genuinely fit, coordinate efficiently, and support you through to successful joining. No fluff, no delays.

Our Process

How We Fill Your Requirement

A structured, four-step hiring process designed to reduce time-to-fill and improve candidate quality at every stage.

1
Share Requirement
You share the role details, team size, location, and specific criteria. We ask the right questions upfront to avoid wasted cycles.
2
Candidate Screening
We source, evaluate, and shortlist candidates against your JD before sharing any profiles with your team.
3
Interview Coordination
We manage scheduling, handle availability, and follow up with candidates through each round — so your team focuses on evaluating, not coordinating.
4
Successful Joining
We support the offer and pre-joining stage to ensure candidates show up and join — reducing last-minute drop-offs.
